# BG12J — Coach-Mark Onboarding

Status: **ACTIVE**  
Started: 6 September 2026  
Base: `80c0b35bcd1a1e3d6c1e6915b61574a59d6a1791`

## Purpose

Convert the existing guided campaign presentation into compact, anchored coach marks so onboarding teaches the board in context instead of covering it with a large tutorial surface.

BG12J is a presentation and usability package. It does not rewrite tutorial progression, campaign rules, board state, map geography or the renderer.

## Locked authority boundaries

BG12J must preserve the existing tutorial and board seams:

- `TutorialOverlay` remains the guided campaign presentation component;
- `TUTORIAL_STEPS`, `getTutorialStep`, `moveTutorial`, `progressTutorial`, `restartTutorial` and `skipTutorial` remain the tutorial state/progression authority;
- the existing explanation-phase sequencing remains intact;
- existing target selectors and `tutorialAnchorSelector` remain the source of contextual anchoring;
- MapLibre retains map lifecycle, camera, terrain and geographic projection ownership;
- BG12H/BG12I retain formation interaction and board-token ownership.

BG12J must not:

- add tutorial-specific gameplay rules or RNG;
- create a second tutorial state machine;
- auto-complete action-driven tutorial steps without the existing game confirmation;
- introduce a modal backdrop that blocks ordinary board interaction;
- replace MapLibre camera/terrain ownership;
- implement the later BG12K general secondary-drawer architecture.

## Presentation contract

### Coach marks

- Guidance remains anchored to the relevant board/control target where one exists.
- The highlighted target remains visually identifiable without darkening the whole board.
- The coach surface is materially smaller than the previous tutorial panel.
- Progress, title, instruction, completion context, navigation and skip remain available.
- Long content may scroll inside the compact coach surface rather than expanding over the board.
- The surrounding board remains visible and usable because the guide container and spotlight do not intercept pointer input; only the coach card itself is interactive.

### Desktop budget

At approximately 1900×829 and 1366×768:

- coach mark width: at most 320 px;
- coach mark height: at most 310 px;
- no full-screen dim/scrim generated by the tutorial spotlight;
- horizontal overflow: at most 2 px;
- anchored target remains visible;
- the map remains the dominant surface.

### Compact budget

At approximately 640×900:

- coach mark width: at most 308 px;
- coach mark height: at most 280 px;
- top/bottom docking remains available to avoid covering the requested target;
- horizontal overflow: at most 2 px;
- the board remains visible around the coach mark.

## Accessibility

- Existing `aria-live="polite"` and tutorial label are retained.
- Coach controls remain native buttons and keyboard operable.
- Target meaning is not conveyed by colour alone: the focused control itself remains visible while the ring provides supplementary emphasis.
- Reduced-motion users do not receive the coach-ring pulse animation.
- Existing tutorial replay/skip storage behaviour is unchanged.

## Validation

Required before manual acceptance:

1. source contracts proving existing tutorial state/progression authority is retained;
2. legacy tutorial/onboarding tests remain green;
3. full project regression suite;
4. production build;
5. desktop browser evidence at approximately 1900×829 and 1366×768;
6. compact browser evidence at approximately 640×900;
7. coach mark meets the desktop/compact size budgets;
8. no full-screen tutorial dimming/scrim;
9. target remains anchored/highlighted and the rest of the board remains visible;
10. tutorial controls remain usable and action-driven progression remains unchanged;
11. no more than 2 px horizontal overflow;
12. exact-head screenshots are manually reviewed before merge.

## Manual gate

BG12J does not merge until the user confirms that onboarding now feels like contextual board guidance rather than a tutorial screen, and that the coach marks are small enough not to interfere with ordinary play.
